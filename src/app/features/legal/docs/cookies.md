This Cookie Policy explains which cookies and similar technologies {{productName}} uses, on our own website and inside the widgets our customers embed on theirs. It supplements our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy).

## 1. Our website and account area

We keep cookies to a minimum. Strictly necessary cookies are always on because the site cannot work without them. Analytics cookies are **off until you opt in**, and nothing is sent to Google before you do.

| Cookie | Set by | Purpose | Category | Lasts |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Session cookie | Our API | Keeps you signed in. `HttpOnly`, `Secure`, `SameSite=Lax` | Strictly necessary | Until you sign out, or the session expires |
| `linker_consent` | Us | Remembers your cookie choice (a version number, one yes/no flag and a timestamp — nothing personal) | Strictly necessary | 12 months |
| `kit_lang` | Us | Remembers the language you picked | Strictly necessary | 12 months |
| `_ga` | Google Analytics | Distinguishes visitors with a random identifier | Analytics — **opt-in** | Up to 2 years |
| `_ga_<container-id>` | Google Analytics | Keeps session state for analytics | Analytics — **opt-in** | Up to 2 years |

**Google Analytics** runs only on our marketing pages, never inside the editor. Google signals and advertising features are turned off, and we never send names or email addresses to it.

We load our fonts from our own servers, not from a third-party font service, so viewing our pages does not send your IP address to a font provider.

## 2. Your choices

- **Cookie settings** in the footer reopens the consent panel at any time. Withdrawing consent deletes the analytics cookies.
- **Global Privacy Control.** If your browser sends a GPC signal, we treat it as an opt-out and keep analytics off.
- You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking the session cookie will prevent you from signing in.

## 3. Widgets on our customers' websites

When a website owner embeds a {{productName}} widget, our script runs on **their** website. We designed it to collect as little as possible:

- **No cookies.** Widgets do not set cookies, and they do not read cookies set by others.
- **No cross-site tracking.** Widgets do not use advertising identifiers, tracking pixels or device fingerprinting, and do not follow visitors across websites.
- **Local storage for display rules.** A widget may store small values in the visitor's browser (keys starting with `kit_`) to respect the rules the website owner set, such as "show this popup once per visitor" or "don't show again after the form was sent". These values never leave the browser. Website owners can turn this off for any widget.
- **Delivery data.** Like any web request, loading a widget sends the visitor's IP address and browser details to our delivery servers. We use them only to deliver the widget, protect the Service from abuse and derive a coarse country for statistics; we do not store IP addresses alongside widget statistics.
- **Widget statistics.** We count views, clicks and form submissions per widget so the website owner can see what works. These counts are not tied to a visitor identifier.
- **Forms.** Anything a visitor types into a widget form is sent to us and stored for the website owner, who decides what the form asks for and why.

The **website owner** decides whether their use of widgets requires consent in their visitors' country and is responsible for their own cookie banner and privacy notice. For widget data, we act as the website owner's processor under our [Data Processing Agreement](/legal/dpa).
