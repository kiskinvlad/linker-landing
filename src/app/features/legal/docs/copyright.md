We respect intellectual-property rights and expect our customers to do the same. This policy explains how to report content in a {{productName}} widget that you believe infringes your copyright or trademark, and how customers can respond. It is part of our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms).

## 1. Who owns what

- **Customers own their content.** Text, images, logos, videos and other material customers upload remain theirs (or their licensors'). Customers promise in the Terms that they have every right needed to use it.
- **We own the platform.** The {{productName}} software, widget engine, SDK, APIs, templates, interface design, documentation and our name and logos belong to {{legalEntity}}. Customers may use templates only inside widgets built with the Service.

## 2. Reporting copyright infringement {#notice}

Send a written notice to our designated agent:

**Copyright Agent**, {{legalEntity}}, {{legalAddress}}
Email: **{{copyrightEmail}}**

Your notice must include:

1. your physical or electronic signature, as the owner or a person authorised to act for the owner;
2. identification of the copyrighted work you claim is infringed (or a representative list, if there are several);
3. identification of the infringing material and enough information for us to find it — ideally the URL of the page showing the widget and a screenshot;
4. your name, postal address, telephone number and email address;
5. a statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use is not authorised by the owner, its agent or the law;
6. a statement that the information in the notice is accurate and, **under penalty of perjury**, that you are the owner or are authorised to act for the owner.

This format follows the US Digital Millennium Copyright Act (17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3)), but we accept notices from any country.

**Be accurate.** Under 17 U.S.C. § 512(f) and similar laws elsewhere, a person who knowingly misrepresents that material is infringing may be liable for damages. Consider whether the use may be fair use or otherwise permitted before you send a notice.

## 3. What happens next

When we receive a complete notice, we will promptly disable or remove the material, which usually means disabling the widget, and forward the notice (with your contact details) to the customer. Incomplete notices may not be acted on; we will tell you what is missing.

## 4. Counter-notice

If you are a customer and believe the material was removed by mistake or misidentification, send a counter-notice to {{copyrightEmail}} including:

1. your physical or electronic signature;
2. identification of the material removed and where it appeared before removal;
3. a statement, **under penalty of perjury**, that you have a good-faith belief the material was removed or disabled as a result of mistake or misidentification;
4. your name, address and telephone number, and a statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the federal district court for the judicial district in which your address is located (or, if your address is outside the United States, any judicial district in which we may be found), and that you will accept service of process from the person who sent the original notice or their agent.

We will forward the counter-notice to the complainant. Unless they tell us within **10 business days** that they have filed a court action to restrain the customer, we will restore the material within **10–14 business days** after receiving the counter-notice.

## 5. Repeat infringers

We close the accounts of customers who are repeat infringers in appropriate circumstances. As a rule, an account that receives **three** valid, uncontested notices within 12 months will be closed.

## 6. Trademarks and other rights

For trademark infringement, counterfeits, impersonation or other rights (such as privacy or publicity rights), email {{copyrightEmail}} with your contact details, the right you hold (for a trademark, the registration number and country), the URL and a screenshot of the widget, and why you believe it infringes. We review these reports under our [Acceptable Use Policy](/legal/acceptable-use) and may disable the widget and notify the customer.

## 7. Other illegal content

To report content that is illegal for reasons other than intellectual property, use the process in our [Acceptable Use Policy](/legal/acceptable-use#reporting).
