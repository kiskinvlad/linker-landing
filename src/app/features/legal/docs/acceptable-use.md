- malicious or deceptive redirects, clickjacking, or content that disguises where a button or link leads;
- scams, pyramid or Ponzi schemes, fake giveaways, fake prizes ("You've won a free iPhone!") and advance-fee schemes;
- impersonation of any person, brand, government body or organisation.

**Deceptive and unlawful marketing**

- false, misleading or unsubstantiated claims about products, prices, discounts, results, earnings, health effects or scarcity — for example fake countdown timers or fake "only 2 left" messages;
- fake or undisclosed paid reviews, testimonials or endorsements;
- "guaranteed" investment returns or other misleading financial promises;
- unsolicited bulk messages (spam), or collecting email addresses or phone numbers for marketing without the consent the law requires.

**Illegal goods, services and content**

- illegal drugs and drug paraphernalia, and unlawful products or services of any kind;
- counterfeit goods, or content that infringes copyright, trademarks or other rights;
- content that sexually exploits children, and any sexually explicit content;

- illegal gambling;
- weapons, ammunition, explosives and their components;
- goods or services subject to sanctions, or dealings with sanctioned persons or regions.

**Privacy violations**

- collecting special-category data (health, biometric or genetic data, racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, religious beliefs, trade-union membership, sex life or sexual orientation), government ID numbers, full payment-card numbers or passwords through widget forms;
- collecting personal data from children under 16 (or under the age set by local law, such as 13 under COPPA in the US);
- covert tracking of visitors, fingerprinting, or collecting data without the notice or consent the law requires.

**Political content**

- political advertising or campaign material, including content promoting candidates, parties, ballot measures or referendums, or soliciting political donations.

**Abusing the Service**

- probing, scanning or testing the Service's security without our written permission (report vulnerabilities to {{securityEmail}});
- circumventing plan limits, rate limits, usage quotas or access controls, including by creating multiple free accounts;
- overloading the Service, interfering with other customers, or reverse-engineering it except where the law expressly allows;
