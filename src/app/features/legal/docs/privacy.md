## 7. How we protect it

All traffic is encrypted with TLS. Passwords are hashed with Argon2id; sessions are server-side and the session cookie is `HttpOnly` and `Secure`; verification and reset tokens are stored hashed; IP addresses in acceptance records are hashed. Data at rest is encrypted by our hosting provider. Access to production systems is limited to people who need it and protected by multi-factor authentication. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your personal data we will notify you and the competent authorities as the law requires.
