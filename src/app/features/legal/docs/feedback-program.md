The {{productName}} Feedback Program rewards customers whose ideas make the product better. These rules are part of our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms). Submissions are judged by the version of these rules in force on the day they were sent.

## 1. What you can get

If you suggest a feature and we ship it **as a feature of a paid plan**, we unlock that feature on **your account, free of charge, on any plan — including Free** — for as long as your account stays active.

Everything else we ship from your feedback reaches every customer anyway, so it earns a **"Shipped — thank you"** status and an email, not an unlock. This covers:

- features that ship to the Free plan or to every plan;
- improvements to something you already have access to;
- bug fixes.

## 2. Plan limits are never unlocked

Rewards unlock on/off capabilities only. **Quotas and limits** — number of widgets, monthly views, versions per widget and any other usage limit — always follow your plan, even if your suggestion was "give me more" of something.

## 3. Who can take part

You need a {{productName}} account with a verified email address. Submit ideas through the feedback form while signed in, so the reward has an owner.

## 4. How ideas are reviewed

1. **Submitted** — we have your idea.
2. **Under review** — we are assessing it.
3. **Approved** — we have decided to build it. The date of approval matters for duplicates (section 5).
4. **Shipped** — it is live. You see "Shipped — free for you" if it unlocked a paid feature on your account, or "Shipped — thank you" otherwise.

An idea may instead be marked **Declined** or **Duplicate**. We decide at our discretion what to build, when, and in which plan it ships; approval is a plan to build, not a promise of a date or a reward type. Whether an unlock applies is decided when the feature ships.

## 5. Duplicates

Everyone who submitted the same idea **before it was approved** receives the same reward. Ideas submitted after approval are marked "already planned" and do not earn a reward.

## 6. Rewards are personal

Rewards belong to the account that submitted the idea. They cannot be transferred, sold or exchanged for money, and they end if the account is deleted. If a deleted account is restored within our deletion window, its rewards come back. We may remove rewards from accounts closed for breaching the Terms.

## 7. What you give us

By submitting feedback you grant us a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free licence to use, modify and build on it for any purpose. Submitting creates no ownership, payment or other claim beyond the reward described here. Don't include personal data about other people or confidential information in your submission.

## 8. Changes to the program

We may change or end the program. Changes apply only to ideas submitted after the change: ideas already approved under earlier rules are honoured, and rewards already granted stay in place for as long as the account is active.
